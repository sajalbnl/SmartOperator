# Tool Change

1. Stop the cycle and move the spindle to the tool-change position.
2. Confirm the replacement tool number, holder, and offset sheet.
3. Clean the holder taper and spindle taper with a lint-free cloth.
4. Load the holder and confirm the retention knob seats fully.
5. Enter measured length and diameter offsets at the control.
6. Run the first approach in single-block at 25% rapid.
