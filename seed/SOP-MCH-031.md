# Chip Clearing

1. Stop the cycle, stop the spindle, and isolate automatic motion.
2. Wear eye protection and cut-resistant gloves.
3. Pull long swarf with a chip hook; keep hands out of the chip pile.
4. Brush chips from the table, guards, and probe cover toward the conveyor.
5. Do not use compressed air on spindle seals, guideways, or electrical cabinets.
6. Close the enclosure and run the conveyor until the trough is clear.
