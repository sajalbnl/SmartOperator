# Daily Lubrication Check

1. Stop the spindle and park all axes at the service position.
2. Check way-lube level through the reservoir sight glass.
3. Top up with ISO VG 68 way oil if the level is below MIN.
4. Inspect lines and metering blocks for leaks or pinched tubing.
5. Run one manual lube cycle and confirm pressure rises, then returns.
6. Wipe spills and record the quantity added in the shift log.
