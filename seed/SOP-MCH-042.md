# Spindle Vibration Troubleshooting

1. Press feed hold, stop the spindle, and remove the workpiece.
2. Remove the toolholder and inspect the taper, retention knob, and holder for damage.
3. Clean both tapers, fit a known-good balanced holder, and test at 1,000 rpm.
4. Repeat at 3,000 rpm and 6,000 rpm; note where vibration begins.
5. If vibration remains with the known-good holder, lock out the machine and call maintenance.
6. Inspect the spindle bearing for roughness, axial play, heat marks, or noise while turning.
7. Replace the spindle bearing if wear or play is outside the service limit.
8. Reassemble, run the spindle warmup, and verify vibration at all three speeds before release.
