# Toolholder Runout Check

1. Stop the spindle and clean the spindle taper, holder taper, and collet.
2. Fit a certified test bar and tighten to the holder maker's torque.
3. Mount a dial indicator on a rigid base.
4. Measure runout 10 mm from the nose, then near the end of the bar.
5. Rotate the spindle by hand for two full turns and record total indicator movement.
6. Quarantine the holder if runout exceeds the setup-sheet limit.
