# End-of-Shift Shutdown

1. Finish the active cycle and remove the part and tools left in the spindle.
2. Clear loose chips from the enclosure and empty the chip basket.
3. Wipe the table, probe, and exposed fixture faces.
4. Park the axes at the marked shutdown position.
5. Check for active alarms, leaks, or damaged tooling and note them in the shift log.
6. Press power off at the control, then switch off the main isolator.
